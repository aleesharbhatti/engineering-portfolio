Rocket Design & Launch Fabrication

SolidWorks | 3D Printing | Laser Cutting | Aerodynamics & Flight Stability

This project involved designing, modeling, and fabricating a functional multi-stage high-stability rocket built from the ground up to achieve maximum flight altitude and controlled descent recovery.

  • Aerodynamic Surface Modeling: Generated von Kármán nose cone profiles and swept fin geometries in SolidWorks to minimize atmospheric drag and keep the Center of Pressure (CP) strictly behind the Center of Gravity (CG) during flight.
  • CAD & Tolerance Design: Designed inter-stage coupler rings, payload bays, and motor retainer clips in CAD with tight fit tolerances to account for material thermal expansion during launch ignition.
  • Additive & Subtractive Fabrication: Precision laser-cut interior mounting bulkheads and 3D-printed lightweight PETG nose sections to handle high kinetic impact upon landing.
  • Hardware Integration & Flight Tests: Hand-assembled launch rail buttons, shock cords, and recovery deployment systems, running ground ejection tests to ensure reliable parachute deployment.

Deviated Septum Correction Device

Nitinol Shape-Memory Alloy | Silicone Molding | Biocompatible Prototyping

Designed a non-surgical proof-of-concept medical device for deviated nasal septum correction utilizing thermally activated shape-memory alloys embedded within soft elastomeric casings.

  • Shape-Memory Alloy Integration: Configured heat-treated Nitinol wire structures programmed to activate at human body temperature, providing controlled and continuous outward spring tension against nasal cartilage.
  • 3D Mold Design & Silicone Casting: Modeled custom negative molds in SolidWorks and poured dual-component biocompatible silicone to fully encapsulate the metal frame and prevent soft tissue abrasion.
  • Mechanical Force Testing: Evaluated physical revisions under compression setups, verifying that outward mechanical forces stayed within safe biomechanical parameters for patient comfort.
  • Hardware Iteration: Refined mold tolerances and wire gauge configurations across multiple test prints to maximize structural memory recovery and ergonomic fit.

Truss Bridge Design & Structural FEA

Structural Mechanics | Finite Element Analysis | Destructive Testing

Executed full structural design, FEA stress modeling, and physical destructive load testing for a high-efficiency truss bridge optimized for load-to-weight performance.

  • Hand Calculations & Joint Analysis: Analyzed member tension and compression forces using the method of joints and sections to determine ideal truss geometry and strut dimensions.
  • SolidWorks Simulation (FEA): Conducted static linear stress analyses and buckling simulations in SolidWorks FEA to locate peak stress concentrations and eliminate unnecessary material weight.
  • Precision Construction: Machined structural members and assembled joints with rigid gusset plates to ensure accurate transfer of vertical static forces straight to support abutments.
  • Empirical Destructive Testing: Loaded the physical bridge to failure on a hydraulic load frame, correlating physical fracture points directly against FEA predictions to validate calculations.

Automated Cardiovascular Heart Stent

Cardiovascular Engineering | Cyclic Fatigue FEA | Medical Device Design

Engineered an expandable mesh cardiovascular stent system engineered to alleviate arterial stenosis and hypertension while maintaining high radial strength and long-term fatigue resistance.

  • Parametric Strut CAD Geometry: Modeled repeating balloon-expandable mesh struts in SolidWorks, balancing outer radial expansion ratio with minimal axial foreshortening.
  • Non-Linear FEA Fatigue Analysis: Performed non-linear material stress simulations to evaluate plastic deformation during deployment and verify structural fatigue limits under cyclic hemodynamic pressure.
  • Fluid Dynamics & Hemodynamics: Optimized inner surface profiles to reduce fluid recirculation and wall shear stress, helping minimize blood turbulence and thrombosis risks.
  • Medical Design Compliance: Applied medical device design rules to maintain continuous outer wall contact without inducing arterial wall puncture or excessive inflammation.

CO₂ Pressurized Wooden Dragster

Aerodynamic Mass Reduction | Precision Machining | Track Testing

Designed, machined, and aerodynamically refined a low-mass wooden dragster engineered to achieve peak acceleration on a guided track using pressurized CO₂ cartridge thrust.

  • Chassis Shaping & Drag Reduction: Shaped a balsa wood chassis to minimize frontal cross-sectional area and reduce turbulent air drag along the rear pressure chamber.
  • Precision Machining & Lathe Work: Machined custom brass bushings and turned lightweight aluminum axles on a lathe to guarantee low friction and straight-line tracking.
  • Weight Distribution & Center of Mass: Drilled internal weight-reduction pockets around non-structural areas, balancing chassis mass directly over the rear wheels to maximize initial traction.
  • High-Speed Track Trials: Measured race performance using optical timer sensors, adjusting wheel alignment tolerances to eliminate wobble and drag during top-speed runs.

Thermodynamic Phase-Change Surface Model

Thermodynamics | CAD Surface Modeling | 3D Printing & Slicing

Constructed a physical, 3D mathematical representation of water's thermodynamic state surface (P-V-T) to visually and tactitly demonstrate phase boundaries across solid, liquid, and vapor regions.

  • Mathematical Surface Extraction: Converted thermodynamic state equations and empirical phase diagram data points into a continuous 3D surface mesh inside CAD.
  • Phase Boundary Feature Mapping: Extruded physical ridges along the sublimation, melting, and vaporization lines, highlighting the triple point and critical point for clear visual analysis.
  • Additive Manufacturing & Slicing: Optimized print orientation, layer heights, and support structures in Ultimaker Cura to ensure smooth surface slopes without steep overhang defects.
  • Technical Report & Presentation: Authored an accompanying technical thermodynamics report detailing state function derivations and phase transition properties mapped on the model.
